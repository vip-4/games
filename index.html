Key facts

  • 142 browser games, playable instantly with no installation or signup.
  • Built with Phaser 3, Three.js and vanilla JavaScript.
  • Static site with no build step required for hosting.
  • Searchable and filterable game catalog with category counts.

Frequently asked questions

What is Games Hub?

A curated collection of 142 playable browser games. You can click and play instantly without installing anything or creating an account.

Which technologies are used?

Games are built with Phaser 3, Three.js and vanilla JavaScript. The whole collection is static and requires no build step to host.

Do I need to install or sign up?

No. Every game runs in the browser. Open the hub, pick a game and play immediately.

Can I play on mobile?

Yes. The hub is responsive and the games are browser-based, so they work on desktop and mobile browsers.

Is the collection open source?

Yes. The project is published under the MIT License and the source is available on GitHub.
